Chart controls
  • Drag across the chart to select a time range. Drag the amber edges to adjust it. Select whole log takes everything, for saving the full log without the unticked message types.
  • Scroll to zoom around the pointer. Double-click to show the whole log. Drag the strip under the chart to pan.
  • Click a numbered bar in the top row, or a row in the parts list, to load that part into the selection.
  • Cuts snap to message boundaries every 32 KB of log, which is a few tens of milliseconds at full logging rate.

Message types

Sorted by share of the file. Untick a type to leave it out of everything you save. Types logged per sensor show ▸; open it to untick single instances. Instances are numbered as in the log, from 0, so IMU[3] is the fourth IMU. Raw IMU and batch-sampler data (IMU, GYR, ACC, ISBD) are often most of a full-logging file.
